Joining Us

Applying to MMMI Lab

If you are interested in joining our lab, please send your CV to h.ryu(at)hufs.ac.kr with the title [Application Type] Your Full Name (e.g., [MS Application] Hong Gildong or [Undergraduate Intern Application] John Doe).

Undergraduate Intern

We welcome applications from undergraduate students for internship positions. Please include your academic transcript when applying. English proficiency scores are preferred but not required. The internship program consists of two stages: a deep learning fundamentals study track followed by a research track. Applicants with sufficient background may be exempted from the fundamentals study track after an interview.

Graduate Students

Graduate student applicants must include their GPA and English proficiency score on their CV. Applicants who have previously completed an internship at the MMMI Lab will be given higher priority.
